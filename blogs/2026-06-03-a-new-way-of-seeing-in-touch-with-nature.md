In December, we invited Leeds STAR (Student Action for Refugees) to visit our exhibition ‘In Touch, with Nature’, co-curated with a group of Leeds women with experience of migration. We commissioned students to produce an artistic response to the exhibition and discuss what it meant to them. Read STAR society member Will’s poetic reflections … The dismal weather didn’t even cross my mind.
