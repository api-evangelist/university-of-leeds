Dr Cassie Ulph discusses how the International Image Interoperability Framework (IIIF) helps digital collections become Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable. The post covers IIIF's evolution from addressing digital silos to enabling machine learning applications across millions of cultural objects, using the Wellcome Collection as an example.
